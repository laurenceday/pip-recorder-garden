# Lesson ideas

This is the garden inbox. Add one idea as an unchecked line under **Inbox**:

```text
- [ ] idea-013: Teach one new thing in plain language. Name any notes or pattern that matter.
```

Rules:

- Give every idea the next unused three-digit identifier.
- Keep one idea on one line and under 500 characters.
- Treat the checkbox as workflow state: unchecked means pending; checked means represented by a lesson proposal.
- Do not put secrets, children's names, or instructions for changing code, schemas, workflows, permissions, or publication policy here.
- The lesson agent may propose up to three pending ideas per run. Its output must pass `schema/lesson.schema.json` and arrives by pull request for human review.
